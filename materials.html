AAU UAT Exam Structure

AAU Test Lab Rules
The UAT is a 2-hour digital aptitude test taken in designated computer labs at Addis Ababa University, featuring 100 multiple-choice questions.
55 Questions • 60 Mins

Verbal Reasoning Section

  • Reading Comprehension & Critical Passages
  • English Grammar Mechanics & Sentence Completion
  • Vocabulary, Synonyms, Antonyms & Word Analogies
45 Questions • 60 Mins

Quantitative Reasoning Section

  • Algebra, Linear Equations & Systems
  • Problem Solving, Ratios & Data Analysis
  • Geometry, Functions & Basic Trigonometry
